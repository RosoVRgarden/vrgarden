VRgarden JROTools

The 17 volumes of the JROTools Hard-Surface Kitbash series, sold on Gumroad. Each volume is a set of mid-poly parts you can use as they are, with soft normals, in real time, or subdivide for higher quality. All volumes are also included in the Hard-Surface Kitbash Collection and in the 3D Artists Tools & Assets Mega Bundle. Each card links to the volume’s Gumroad page.
