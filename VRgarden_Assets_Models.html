VRgarden models

This library contains 3D models and environments (buildings, cities, interiors and props) from the Unity Asset Store and Fab. Each card links to the asset’s store page and, where available, its documentation. Cards also show details such as the version and any compatibility issues. Essentials are the assets we use most often in the course.
