VRgarden Architecture Studio

Reference libraries for the VRgarden Architecture Studio at Feng-Chia University. It contains asset libraries such as models, nature elements and world building from the Unity asset store and Fab.com, Reallusion characters, the animation library, the sound library, the trees and plants in Taiwan and workflows, interaction and finishing tools.
