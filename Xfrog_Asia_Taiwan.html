Trees and plants in Taiwan

VRgarden's tree and plant models: Xfrog Asia pack, trees in Unity packs and other CGTrader assets. They are sorted by whether and where each grows in Taiwan. Photos load from Wikipedia; each card links to the pack that contains it.

Taiwan status, settings and native regions are a broad classification from general botanical knowledge, not an official survey. Photos show the real species (or its genus when no species photo exists) and come from Wikipedia under their own licences; the Hachiku Bamboo image is Xfrog's own render. Species list from the Xfrog Asia pack page on CGTrader, October 2026. Unity and CGTrader entries come from your tree correspondence sheet; the Oasis trees are assumed to be from Unity’s URP 3D Sample.
