VRgarden stamping

Terrain stamps (heightmaps) from Rowlan’s StampIT! collection on the Unity Asset Store, for sculpting terrain with Unity Terrain Tools, MicroVerse or Gaia. Each card links to the pack’s store page and shows its version. Packs are grouped by the kind of landscape they create.
